## What CreatorOps implements instead

The new, separate **GTA VI Clips Lab** does not download YouTube/Twitch/Kick videos, capture live third-party streams, scrape streamer chat, infer another creator's private retention, or auto-publish. Before the announced release date, its API also requires a written pre-release authorization reference; leaked footage is prohibited. It accepts a local video file that the operator already owns or is explicitly authorized to use, optionally ranks abrupt frame-change windows as non-semantic cues for human review, trims a manually selected 5–60 second segment, creates a 1080×1920 vertical render, and writes manually supplied Hindi/Hinglish captions to an evenly timed draft SRT. The SRT timing is a rough draft, not speech recognition; the user must correct it. Optional Hindi VO goes through the already configured network voice provider and may have cost/terms implications. Other platforms receive copy variants only; YouTube is the only uploader in CreatorOps.

The upload/publish path remains fail-closed. It requires separate source-recording rights and case-specific written commercial permission for Take-Two/Rockstar game IP, plus a human confirmation of original commentary, rights for all audio/music, no leaked or prohibited material, fact-check, render QA, approval and account/API gates. This workflow does not verify that an entered license reference is authentic, provide legal advice, guarantee YouTube monetization, or promise reach/revenue.

## Official rules used in the design

- Rockstar's current policy says it generally does not object to fan material for **non-commercial** use, reserves the right to remove it, prohibits leaked pre-release footage and several spoiler/restricted-content categories, and invites case-by-case licensing inquiries. This is why the GTA VI workflow does not treat a user's recording or a streamer's permission as commercial permission for the game IP: [Rockstar Games Customer Support — Policy on posting copyrighted Rockstar Games material](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/7bNaeoMFTV0iUDGhStTXvz/policy-on-posting-copyrighted-rockstar-games-material).
- YouTube's reused-content monetization review is distinct from copyright permission and can apply even when the uploader has permission. Meaningful original commentary/editing and channel-level originality matter: [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en-in).
- YouTube says credit alone is not a permission grant: [Common copyright myths](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797449?hl=en). Its [Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms?hl=en) restrict use/download of Service content except where the Service permits it, the rights holders authorize it, or applicable law allows it; the new tool has no YouTube downloader.
- Twitch's [official Clips API](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/api/clips/) supports clip creation and retrieval, but an API endpoint is not a source-rights check. CreatorOps does not call Twitch or download VODs/clips; a separate official API/terms and rights review would be needed before considering such an integration.
- Rockstar's official release announcement states November 19, 2026: [GTA VI release announcement](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026). From 2026-09-30 to that date is 50 full days (51 calendar dates if counting September 30 as day one).
