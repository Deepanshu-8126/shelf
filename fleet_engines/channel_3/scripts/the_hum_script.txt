Duniya mein 2 lakh log aise hain, jo ek aisi awaaz sunte hain, jo koi aur nahi sunta. Na doctor samjha paaye, na science explain kar paaye. Ise kehte hain The Hum.

Saal 1999. Arizona, USA. Ek chhota sa sheher St. George. Achanak logon ko ek ajeeb si awaaz sunai dene lagi. Jaise koi bohot door se engine chal raha ho. Bina kisi source ke. Bina kisi direction ke. Bas ek low frequency hum. 30 hertz. Insaan ke kaan ke liye almost invisible.

Phir ye phenomenon UK mein aaya. Bristol 2001. Phir New Zealand. Phir Australia. Phir Canada. Har sheher mein alag log, alag frequency, alag time. Par ek hi cheez common: jo sunta hai, uski neend khatam. Headache, darr. Kuch logon ko lage ki koi unhe follow kar raha hai. Aur sabse darawani baat: jo Hum sunte hain, unhe pata bhi nahi chalta ki wo sun rahe hain, jab tak wo ek aisi jagah nahi jaate jahan Hum nahi hai. Wahan pehli baar unhe samajh aata hai ki kuch galat hai.

Science ne kya kaha? Ek theory hai infrasound. 20 hertz se neeche ki awaaz, jo hawa mein travel karti hai, deewaron se guzar jaati hai, aur insaan ke seedha inner ear mein jaati hai. Kuch log sensitive hote hain, unhe sunai deta hai. Par problem ye hai: jab researchers ne instruments se measure kiya, toh koi signal nahi mila, koi explanation nahi mili. 25 saal ho gaye, abhi bhi unsolved.

Par sabse darawani baat ye hai: 2023 mein ek study aayi. Jo Hum sunte hain, unka amygdala, yaani darr wala part, 40 percent zyada active hai. Matlab Hum sirf awaaz nahi hai, ye dimaag ko directly affect karta hai. Aur jo log sunna shuru karte hain, unhe 6 mahine baad band nahi hota, kabhi nahi hota.

Ab main tumse ek sawaal poochta hoon. Kya tumhe abhi koi awaaz sunai de rahi hai? Chhoti si low frequency, jaise door se engine chal raha ho. Agar haan, toh shayad tum Hum ke victim ho. Aur agar nahi, toh chinta mat karo, abhi nahi. Kyunki Hum achanak shuru hota hai, aur jab shuru hota hai, toh kabhi nahi jaata. Good night.
